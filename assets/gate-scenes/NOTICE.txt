Portfolio scene assets

철과 묵. Original PortalDoor, SteamBag, Gun, SteamGlassCover and portal VFX geometry / density texture from Project-SteamAttack.
https://github.com/Shaun1667/Project-SteamAttack

The Cinderella prototype character and clips were rendered to images. Kachujin and Mixamo animations are credited to Adobe Mixamo.
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

Pale Garden sprites include DarkWolf_2d and the PIPO map tile sheet used by the game.
